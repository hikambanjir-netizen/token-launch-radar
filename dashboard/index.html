How the board prices a protocol with no token

Every implied value is derived from the snapshot's own figures, never from a list of names. Protocols whose token already trades give a market-cap-to-TVL multiple for their category (the median, shrunk toward the global median by sample count and winsorised at 10× it, so one outlier cannot price a whole category; the global median here is —). A pre-token protocol is priced at TVL × its category multiple, adjusted by a momentum factor from its 7-day TVL change (clamped, so one wild week cannot dominate) and a track-record factor from how long it has run with no token. Protocols with a live token are the evidence base — they are counted, never ranked. Click any row for the full arithmetic and its farm plan.
